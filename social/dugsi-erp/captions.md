## LinkedIn

Schools don’t run on Monday–Friday defaults — and parents shouldn’t wait weeks for a report card.

Secondary schools needed classes-first ERP: Saturday–Wednesday calendars, parent SMS attendance, bilingual cards, and term grades that used to take weeks.

We shipped Dugsi ERP for Form 1–4: attendance that triggers SMS within minutes of the morning bell, Somali/English printable cards, fees and payroll in one console.
