## LinkedIn

When a tower fails, the first question is ownership — not the spreadsheet tab.

Telecom and broadcast sites grew for years without a unified spatial registry. Inspections lived on paper. License renewals lagged. Spectrum overlaps lacked a national audit trail.

We built TowerLine on the full Google Maps JavaScript API — clustering, styled maps, InfoWindows — plus a sovereign registry for every mast’s ID, GPS, spectrum, and tenancy. Field tablets capture photo proof offline and score compliance (Classes A–C).

See the case → https://m2btek.com/en/portfolio/towerline
