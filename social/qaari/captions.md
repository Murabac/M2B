## LinkedIn

Qur’an audio shouldn’t buffer out mid-ayah — or force listeners to choose between Somali and Arabic communities.

Recitations were scattered across low-bitrate clips. No reliable offline. No verse-by-verse visual sync for memorization.

We built Xulka Qur'aada: Cloudflare R2 + edge caching, Flutter and web players, and timestamp tools that sync Uthmani text to the reciter line by line.

See the case → https://m2btek.com/en/portfolio/qaari
